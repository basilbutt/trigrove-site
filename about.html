Practical, risk-based security programs designed to work in the real world.

What Trigove does

I help organizations reduce cybersecurity risk and improve audit readiness through clear priorities, lightweight governance, and measurable controls.

  • Assess security posture and identify material gaps
  • Build governance (GRC), vendor risk, and evidence processes
  • Prepare for compliance frameworks like SOC 2 and ISO 27001
  • Provide ongoing leadership through vCISO services

My approach

Security should enable the business. My approach is straightforward: understand the environment, identify the highest-impact risks, and implement controls that teams can maintain.
